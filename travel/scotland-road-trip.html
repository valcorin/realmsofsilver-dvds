Edinburgh • Loch Ness • Isle of Lewis • Isle of Skye • Edinburgh

Trip Overview

Experience the magic of Scotland on this carefully planned 10-day self-drive journey through historic cities, mystical lochs, and breathtaking islands. From Edinburgh's medieval charm to the rugged beauty of the Outer Hebrides and the dramatic landscapes of Skye.

🏰 Edinburgh
🌊 Loch Ness
🏝️ Isle of Lewis
⛰️ Isle of Skye
🏰 Edinburgh
Total Distance: Approximately 850 miles (1,370 km)

💡 Essential Tips

  • Drive on the left side of the road
  • Book ferries to the islands in advance
  • Weather can change quickly - pack layers
  • Fill up with petrol when you can, stations are sparse in the Highlands
  • Consider midges (tiny biting insects) from May to September - bring insect repellent
1

Arrival in Edinburgh

📍 Edinburgh
🚗 Driving: Pick up rental car from Edinburgh Airport or city center

Activities & Attractions:

  • Settle into your accommodation and get oriented
  • Explore Edinburgh Old Town and the Royal Mile
  • Visit Edinburgh Castle (book ahead to skip queues)
  • Walk up Arthur's Seat for panoramic city views
  • Dinner on Grassmarket or in the New Town

🏨 Accommodation:

Stay in Edinburgh city center (Old Town or New Town recommended)

2

Explore Edinburgh

📍 Edinburgh

Activities & Attractions:

  • Visit the Palace of Holyroodhouse (Royal residence)
  • Tour the Real Mary King's Close (underground streets)
  • Explore the National Museum of Scotland (free entry)
  • Walk through Princes Street Gardens
  • Browse shops on Princes Street and George Street
  • Evening whisky tasting at a traditional pub

⭐ Don't Miss:

The Scott Monument offers incredible views if you're willing to climb the 287 steps!

🏨 Accommodation:

Stay in Edinburgh city center

3

Edinburgh to Loch Ness

📍 Inverness / Loch Ness
🚗 Driving: 170 miles (275 km) • Approximately 3.5 hours
Route: Edinburgh → Stirling → Pitlochry → Inverness

En Route & Activities:

  • Stop at Stirling Castle (magnificent hilltop fortress)
  • Visit the Wallace Monument near Stirling
  • Lunch break in Pitlochry (charming Highland town)
  • Drive through Cairngorms National Park
  • Arrive at Loch Ness in late afternoon
  • Visit Urquhart Castle ruins on the loch shore
  • Take an evening boat cruise on Loch Ness (Nessie hunting!)

⭐ Don't Miss:

The drive through the Cairngorms is stunning, especially around the Pass of Drumochter.

🏨 Accommodation:

Stay in Inverness or along Loch Ness (Drumnadrochit area)

4

Explore Loch Ness & Inverness

📍 Loch Ness / Inverness

Activities & Attractions:

  • Morning walk along the Great Glen Way
  • Visit Loch Ness Centre & Exhibition in Drumnadrochit
  • Explore Inverness city center and River Ness
  • Visit Inverness Castle viewpoint
  • Browse Victorian Market for local crafts
  • Walk or cycle along the Caledonian Canal
  • Optional: Visit Culloden Battlefield (historic site from 1746)

⭐ Don't Miss:

The Falls of Foyers - a beautiful waterfall accessed via a woodland walk from the south side of Loch Ness.

🏨 Accommodation:

Stay in Inverness or Loch Ness area

5

Loch Ness to Isle of Lewis

📍 Isle of Lewis (Stornoway)
🚗 Driving + Ferry: 120 miles (195 km) + 2hrs 45mins ferry • Total 6+ hours
Route: Inverness → Ullapool → Ferry to Stornoway
⚠️ Book ferry in advance! (Caledonian MacBrayne)

Journey & Activities:

  • Early start for scenic drive to Ullapool (1.5 hours)
  • Coffee break in Ullapool (picturesque fishing village)
  • Ferry crossing to Stornoway (2hrs 45mins) - watch for dolphins!
  • Arrive in Stornoway, capital of the Outer Hebrides
  • Explore Stornoway town and harbor
  • Visit Lews Castle and its grounds
  • Evening walk along Stornoway harbor

⭐ Don't Miss:

The ferry journey itself is spectacular - stand on deck for views of the Hebridean landscape.

🏨 Accommodation:

Stay in Stornoway or nearby

6

Explore Isle of Lewis

📍 Isle of Lewis
🚗 Driving: Approximately 80 miles touring the island

Activities & Attractions:

  • Visit the Callanish Standing Stones (older than Stonehenge!)
  • Explore the Blackhouse village at Gearrannan
  • Visit the Carloway Broch (ancient Iron Age tower)
  • Drive to Uig for dramatic coastal scenery
  • Walk on the white sand beaches of the west coast
  • Visit the Butt of Lewis lighthouse (most northern point)
  • Learn about Harris Tweed at local weavers

⭐ Don't Miss:

The standing stones at Callanish at sunset or sunrise are absolutely magical. The beaches on the west coast (like Dalmore Beach) are Caribbean-like with turquoise water!

🏨 Accommodation:

Stay in Stornoway or on the west coast

7

Isle of Lewis to Isle of Skye

📍 Isle of Skye
🚗 Driving + Ferry: Ferry 1hr 45mins + 100 miles (160 km) • Total 5+ hours
Route: Stornoway → Ferry to Uig (Skye) → Portree
⚠️ Book ferry in advance! (Caledonian MacBrayne)

Journey & Activities:

  • Ferry from Stornoway to Uig on Skye (1hr 45mins)
  • Drive through dramatic Skye landscapes
  • Visit the Quiraing - stunning rock formations (short hike)
  • Stop at Kilt Rock and Mealt Falls viewpoint
  • Arrive in Portree, the island's colorful harbor town
  • Explore Portree's colorful harbor front
  • Dinner at a local restaurant with fresh seafood

⭐ Don't Miss:

The drive to Portree via the Trotternish Ridge is one of Scotland's most scenic routes.

🏨 Accommodation:

Stay in Portree or nearby (book well in advance - Skye is very popular)

8

Explore Isle of Skye

📍 Isle of Skye
🚗 Driving: Approximately 100 miles touring the island

Activities & Attractions:

  • Visit the Old Man of Storr rock formation (1-2 hour hike)
  • Explore the Fairy Pools near Glen Brittle
  • Visit Dunvegan Castle and Gardens (clan seat)
  • Walk at Neist Point lighthouse (dramatic cliffs)
  • Explore Talisker Distillery for whisky tasting
  • Visit the picturesque village of Elgol
  • Evening in Portree for dinner and drinks

⭐ Don't Miss:

The Fairy Pools are magical - crystal clear blue water cascading down the rocks. Go early morning to avoid crowds.

🏨 Accommodation:

Stay in Portree or nearby

9

Isle of Skye to Edinburgh

📍 Fort William / Edinburgh
🚗 Driving: 210 miles (340 km) • Approximately 5 hours
Route: Skye → Kyle of Lochalsh → Fort William → Edinburgh

Journey & Activities:

  • Cross the Skye Bridge back to the mainland
  • Drive through Glen Shiel and the Five Sisters mountains
  • Optional: Stop at Eilean Donan Castle (iconic castle on an island)
  • Lunch in Fort William (base of Ben Nevis, UK's highest peak)
  • Drive through Glencoe - hauntingly beautiful valley
  • Continue south past Loch Lomond
  • Arrive in Edinburgh in the evening

⭐ Don't Miss:

Eilean Donan Castle is one of Scotland's most photographed castles. Glencoe valley is breathtakingly dramatic with its towering peaks.

🏨 Accommodation:

Stay in Edinburgh

10

Edinburgh & Departure

📍 Edinburgh

Final Day Activities:

  • Morning: Last-minute shopping on Princes Street
  • Visit Scottish Parliament building (modern architecture)
  • Brunch at a traditional café
  • Return rental car at Edinburgh Airport or city center
  • Departure or extend your stay!

⭐ Souvenirs to Take Home:

Scottish whisky, Harris Tweed items, shortbread, Edinburgh rock candy, tartan scarves, and local crafts.

Trip Summary

Total Distance: Approximately 850 miles (1,370 km)

Driving Time: About 25-30 hours total

Ferry Bookings: Ullapool-Stornoway and Stornoway-Uig (Skye)

Best Time to Visit: May-September for best weather, though crowds peak July-August
