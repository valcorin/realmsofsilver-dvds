Embark on an epic 9-month journey around the globe, starting from Boston and traveling westward. This carefully crafted itinerary takes you through all seven continents, with week-long stays in strategic hub locations that offer excellent day-trip opportunities.

Boston Departure & Lima, Peru

📍 Lima, Peru
Week 1

🏠 Base Location: Miraflores District, Lima

Start your world journey by flying south from Boston to Lima, Peru. The Miraflores district offers excellent access to Lima's attractions and is a perfect base for exploring Peru's capital and nearby sites.

Day Trips & Activities:

⭐ Historic Lima Centro

Explore Plaza Mayor, the Cathedral of Lima, and the catacombs of San Francisco Monastery. UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Pachacamac Ruins

Ancient archaeological site 40km south of Lima. Pre-Incan temple complex with stunning desert views.

Barranco District

Bohemian neighborhood known for street art, the Bridge of Sighs, and vibrant nightlife.

Peruvian Cooking Class

Learn to make ceviche, lomo saltado, and other Peruvian classics at a local culinary school.

🏨 Suggested Accommodation:

Hotel in Miraflores with ocean views. Budget: Mid-range boutique hotels.

2

Cusco & Sacred Valley

📍 Cusco, Peru
Week 2

🏠 Base Location: Cusco Historic Center

The ancient Incan capital at 3,400m elevation. Allow time to acclimatize. The historic center provides easy access to the Sacred Valley and Machu Picchu.

Day Trips & Activities:

⭐ Machu Picchu

UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the New Seven Wonders of the World. Take the train from Ollantaytambo for this iconic day trip.

⭐ Sacred Valley Tour

Visit Pisac ruins and market, Ollantaytambo fortress, and the Moray agricultural terraces.

Rainbow Mountain

Vinicunca (Rainbow Mountain) - a challenging high-altitude hike to see the colorful striped mountain.

Cusco City Walking Tour

Sacsayhuaman fortress, Qorikancha temple, San Pedro Market, and colonial architecture.

⭐ Don't Miss:

  • Book Machu Picchu tickets well in advance - they sell out!
  • Try guinea pig (cuy) - a traditional Peruvian delicacy
  • Altitude sickness is real - drink coca tea and take it slow

🏨 Suggested Accommodation:

Boutique hotel in Cusco's San Blas neighborhood with character and charm.

3-4

Bolivia: La Paz & Uyuni Salt Flats

📍 La Paz & Uyuni, Bolivia
Weeks 3-4

🏠 Base Locations: La Paz (Week 3) → Uyuni (Week 4)

Continue south into Bolivia. La Paz is the world's highest capital city, and Uyuni offers access to the otherworldly salt flats.

Day Trips & Activities:

⭐ Salar de Uyuni

World's largest salt flat. Take a multi-day tour including Isla Incahuasi with giant cacti, colored lagoons, and geysers.

Death Road Cycling

Mountain bike down the famous "World's Most Dangerous Road" from La Paz - an adrenaline-pumping experience.

Tiwanaku Ruins

Pre-Columbian archaeological site near La Paz, predating the Incan Empire.

Valle de la Luna

Moon Valley near La Paz with unusual eroded rock formations and hiking trails.

🏨 Suggested Accommodation:

La Paz: Hotel in historic center. Uyuni: Salt hotel for unique experience.

Buenos Aires, Argentina

📍 Buenos Aires, Argentina
Week 5

🏠 Base Location: Palermo or San Telmo District

The "Paris of South America" offers incredible architecture, passionate tango, world-class steak, and vibrant neighborhoods to explore.

Day Trips & Activities:

⭐ Tango Show & Lesson

Experience authentic Argentine tango in San Telmo. Take a lesson and attend an evening milonga.

Tigre Delta

Day trip to the Parana Delta. Explore waterways by boat, visit artisan markets, and enjoy riverside cafes.

Colonia del Sacramento

Ferry across the Rio de la Plata to this charming UNESCO World Heritage town in Uruguay.

La Boca & Recoleta

Visit the colorful Caminito street, Recoleta Cemetery (Eva Peron's grave), and MALBA art museum.

🏨 Suggested Accommodation:

Boutique hotel in Palermo Soho or historic San Telmo apartment.

6

Patagonia: El Calafate

📍 El Calafate, Argentina
Week 6

🏠 Base Location: El Calafate

Gateway to Los Glaciares National Park and the famous Perito Moreno Glacier. Stunning Patagonian landscapes await.

Day Trips & Activities:

⭐ Perito Moreno Glacier

One of the world's most accessible glaciers. Walk the boardwalks to see ice calving, or take an ice trek on the glacier itself.

Upsala & Spegazzini Glaciers

Full-day boat tour on Lago Argentino to see multiple glaciers and icebergs.

El Chalten Day Trip

Hike to see Mount Fitz Roy and Cerro Torre - iconic Patagonian peaks.

Estancia Experience

Visit a traditional Patagonian ranch. Horseback riding, lamb asado, and gaucho culture.

🏨 Suggested Accommodation:

Cozy hotel or cabin with mountain views in El Calafate town.

7-8

Chilean Patagonia & Torres del Paine

📍 Puerto Natales, Chile
Weeks 7-8

🏠 Base Location: Puerto Natales

Gateway to Torres del Paine National Park - one of the world's most spectacular hiking destinations.

Day Trips & Activities:

⭐ Torres del Paine W Trek

Multi-day trek through stunning scenery: the Torres, French Valley, and Grey Glacier. Can also do day hikes.

⭐ Base of the Towers

Challenging day hike to see the iconic three granite towers - the park's most famous view.

Grey Glacier Kayaking

Paddle among icebergs with views of Grey Glacier - an unforgettable experience.

Pumas of Patagonia

Guided wildlife safari to spot pumas, guanacos, and condors in the park.

✈️ Preparing for Antarctica:

From Punta Arenas (near Puerto Natales), you'll depart for your Antarctic expedition. Book Antarctic cruises well in advance - departures typically from Ushuaia, Argentina or Punta Arenas, Chile.

🏨 Suggested Accommodation:

Hostel or hotel in Puerto Natales. For trekking: refugios along the W Trek route.

9-10

Antarctica Expedition

📍 Antarctic Peninsula
Weeks 9-10

🚢 Antarctic Expedition Cruise

A once-in-a-lifetime journey to the world's last wilderness. Most expeditions depart from Ushuaia, Argentina and last 10-14 days including the Drake Passage crossing.

Expedition Highlights:

⭐ Penguin Colonies

Walk among thousands of Gentoo, Chinstrap, and Adelie penguins at various landing sites.

⭐ Zodiac Cruising

Navigate between towering icebergs, spot seals and whales, and explore glacial bays.

Deception Island

Sail into an active volcanic caldera. Visit abandoned whaling stations and swim in geothermally heated waters.

Lemaire Channel

Cruise through "Kodak Alley" - one of the most scenic passages in Antarctica.

Polar Plunge

Take the plunge into Antarctic waters - a rite of passage for polar explorers!

Whale Watching

Spot humpback whales, orcas, and minke whales feeding in Antarctic waters.

⭐ Antarctica Tips:

  • Best time to visit: November-March (Antarctic summer)
  • Book 6-12 months in advance for the best ships and cabins
  • Bring seasickness medication for the Drake Passage
  • Waterproof layers and thermal underwear essential

🚢 Expedition Ship:

Small expedition vessel (100-200 passengers) with expert naturalists and comfortable cabins.

11-12

Rio de Janeiro & Iguazu Falls

📍 Rio de Janeiro & Foz do Iguacu, Brazil
Weeks 11-12

🏠 Base Location: Copacabana/Ipanema (Rio) → Foz do Iguacu

Return to South America's vibrant Brazil. Experience Rio's iconic beaches and Christ the Redeemer, then witness the world's most impressive waterfalls.

Day Trips & Activities:

⭐ Christ the Redeemer

Iconic statue atop Corcovado Mountain. Take the cog train for panoramic views of Rio.

⭐ Iguazu Falls

275 waterfalls spanning 2.7km. Visit both Brazilian and Argentine sides for the complete experience.

Sugarloaf Mountain

Cable car ride to the summit for sunset views over Rio, Copacabana, and Guanabara Bay.

Santa Teresa

Bohemian hillside neighborhood with colonial architecture, art galleries, and the famous Escadaria Selaron steps.

Tijuca Forest

World's largest urban rainforest. Hiking trails, waterfalls, and wildlife within the city.

Ilha Grande

Car-free tropical island paradise. Pristine beaches, snorkeling, and hiking trails.

✈️ Next Destination:

From Sao Paulo or Rio, fly west across the Pacific to Sydney, Australia - your gateway to Oceania!

🏨 Suggested Accommodation:

Rio: Beachfront hotel in Ipanema or Copacabana. Iguazu: Hotel with falls view.

Sydney, Australia

📍 Sydney, NSW, Australia
Week 13

🏠 Base Location: Sydney CBD or Bondi Beach

Australia's largest city offers iconic landmarks, beautiful beaches, and easy access to the Blue Mountains and wine regions.

Day Trips & Activities:

⭐ Sydney Opera House

UNESCO World Heritage Site. Take a guided tour or catch a performance in this architectural masterpiece.

⭐ Sydney Harbour Bridge

Walk across or do the famous BridgeClimb for panoramic harbor views.

Blue Mountains

World Heritage wilderness. See the Three Sisters, take the Scenic Railway, and hike through eucalyptus forests.

Bondi to Coogee Walk

Stunning 6km coastal walk past beaches, cliffs, and ocean pools.

Hunter Valley Wine

Australia's oldest wine region. Sample Semillon and Shiraz at cellar doors.

Taronga Zoo

See native Australian wildlife with stunning Sydney Harbour backdrop.

🏨 Suggested Accommodation:

Hotel in The Rocks for historic charm, or Bondi Beach for surf culture.

14

Great Barrier Reef & Cairns

📍 Cairns, Queensland, Australia
Week 14

🏠 Base Location: Cairns or Port Douglas

Gateway to the Great Barrier Reef and the ancient Daintree Rainforest. Tropical paradise meets natural wonder.

Day Trips & Activities:

⭐ Great Barrier Reef

World's largest coral reef system. Snorkel or dive among 1,500 species of fish and 400 types of coral.

Daintree Rainforest

World's oldest rainforest (180 million years). Crocodile spotting, jungle walks, and Cape Tribulation.

Kuranda Village

Scenic railway and Skyrail cableway through rainforest to a charming mountain village.

Atherton Tablelands

Waterfalls circuit, crater lakes, and platypus spotting opportunities.

🏨 Suggested Accommodation:

Cairns waterfront hotel or Port Douglas resort for upscale experience.

New Zealand: North & South Islands

📍 Auckland & Queenstown, New Zealand
Weeks 15-16

🏠 Base Locations: Auckland (Week 15) → Queenstown (Week 16)

From Auckland's Pacific culture to Queenstown's adventure capital, New Zealand offers dramatic landscapes at every turn.

Day Trips & Activities:

⭐ Hobbiton Movie Set

Visit the real Hobbiton from Lord of the Rings in Matamata. Walking tour of the Shire!

⭐ Milford Sound

New Zealand's most famous fiord. Cruise beneath towering cliffs and waterfalls.

Rotorua Geothermal

Bubbling mud pools, geysers, and Maori cultural experiences. Te Puia or Wai-O-Tapu.

Queenstown Adventures

Bungee jumping (birthplace of bungee!), jet boating, skiing, or skydiving.

Waitomo Caves

Glowworm caves - float through underground rivers lit by thousands of glowworms.

Tongariro Crossing

Epic day hike across volcanic terrain - the "Mordor" of LOTR.

✈️ Next Destination:

Fly from New Zealand to Bali, Indonesia - beginning your Asian adventure!

🏨 Suggested Accommodation:

Auckland: City center hotel. Queenstown: Lakeside accommodation with mountain views.

Bali, Indonesia

📍 Ubud & Seminyak, Bali
Week 17

The Island of the Gods offers ancient temples, terraced rice paddies, yoga retreats, and stunning beaches.

Ubud Monkey Forest

Sacred sanctuary with over 700 Balinese long-tailed macaques among ancient temples.

Nusa Penida Island

Day trip to see Kelingking Beach (T-Rex cliff), Angel's Billabong, and manta rays.

Balinese Cooking Class

Visit local markets and learn to prepare traditional Balinese dishes.

🏨 Suggested Accommodation:

Ubud: Jungle villa with pool. Seminyak: Beach resort.

Vietnam: Ho Chi Minh City & Hoi An

📍 Ho Chi Minh City & Hoi An, Vietnam
Week 18

🏠 Base Locations: HCMC (3 days) → Hoi An (4 days)

Experience Vietnam's contrasts: bustling Saigon's street food scene and the ancient charm of UNESCO-listed Hoi An.

Day Trips & Activities:

⭐ Cu Chi Tunnels

Underground tunnel network used during the Vietnam War. Crawl through actual tunnels.

⭐ Hoi An Ancient Town

UNESCO World Heritage trading port. Japanese Covered Bridge, lantern-lit streets, tailors.

Mekong Delta

Full-day tour to floating markets, coconut candy workshops, and sampan boat rides.

My Son Sanctuary

Ancient Hindu temples of the Champa Kingdom. Vietnam's answer to Angkor Wat.

Street Food Tour

Sample pho, banh mi, com tam, and fresh spring rolls on a guided food tour.

Marble Mountains

Five marble and limestone hills with caves, tunnels, and Buddhist sanctuaries near Da Nang.

🏨 Suggested Accommodation:

HCMC: District 1 hotel. Hoi An: Riverside boutique hotel in Ancient Town.

Thailand: Bangkok & North

📍 Bangkok & Chiang Mai, Thailand
Weeks 19-20

🏠 Base Locations: Bangkok (Week 19) → Chiang Mai (Week 20)

From Bangkok's golden temples and street food to Chiang Mai's mountain temples and elephant sanctuaries.

Day Trips & Activities:

⭐ Grand Palace & Wat Phra Kaew

Bangkok's most sacred temple complex housing the Emerald Buddha. Stunning gold spires.

⭐ Ayutthaya

UNESCO ancient capital. Explore temple ruins, Buddha heads in tree roots, and historic parks.

Floating Markets

Damnoen Saduak or Amphawa floating markets - shop from traditional wooden boats.

Elephant Nature Park

Ethical elephant sanctuary near Chiang Mai. Feed, bathe, and learn about rescued elephants.

Doi Suthep Temple

Golden temple on a mountain overlooking Chiang Mai. 309 steps to the top!

Thai Cooking Class

Market tour and hands-on cooking of pad thai, green curry, and mango sticky rice.

🏨 Suggested Accommodation:

Bangkok: Riverside hotel or near Khao San Road. Chiang Mai: Old City guesthouse.

Japan: Tokyo, Kyoto & Beyond

📍 Tokyo & Kyoto, Japan
Weeks 21-22

🏠 Base Locations: Tokyo (Week 21) → Kyoto (Week 22)

Experience Japan's incredible contrast between ultra-modern Tokyo and traditional Kyoto. The JR Pass makes day trips easy.

Day Trips & Activities:

⭐ Mount Fuji

Day trip to Japan's iconic volcano. Hakone for hot springs and views, or Kawaguchiko for lake reflections.

⭐ Fushimi Inari Shrine

10,000 vermillion torii gates winding up the mountainside in Kyoto. Best at dawn.

⭐ Hiroshima & Miyajima

Peace Memorial, floating torii gate, and powerful history lessons.

Nara Deer Park

Hundreds of bowing deer roam freely among ancient temples. Great day trip from Kyoto.

Tokyo's Districts

Shibuya crossing, Senso-ji temple in Asakusa, Akihabara electronics, Harajuku fashion.

Arashiyama Bamboo Grove

Ethereal bamboo forest walk in Kyoto, plus monkey park and riverside temple district.

⭐ Japan Tips:

  • Get a 14-day JR Pass before arriving - saves money on bullet trains
  • Try an onsen (hot spring bath) - amazing experience!
  • 7-Eleven ATMs are the most reliable for international cards

🏨 Suggested Accommodation:

Tokyo: Shinjuku or Shibuya for access. Kyoto: Gion district or near station. Try a ryokan!

23-24

China: Beijing, Xi'an & Shanghai

📍 Beijing, Xi'an & Shanghai, China
Weeks 23-24

🏠 Base Locations: Beijing (5 days) → Xi'an (3 days) → Shanghai (6 days)

Ancient wonders meet modern megacities. From the Great Wall to the Terracotta Warriors to Shanghai's skyline.

Day Trips & Activities:

⭐ Great Wall of China

Hike the world's longest wall. Mutianyu (restored) or Jinshanling (wilder) sections recommended.

⭐ Terracotta Warriors

8,000 life-sized clay soldiers guarding Emperor Qin's tomb in Xi'an. Mind-blowing!

⭐ Forbidden City

Largest palace complex in the world. 980 buildings and centuries of imperial history.

Temple of Heaven

Beautiful circular temple in Beijing where emperors prayed for good harvests.

The Bund & Pudong

Shanghai's iconic waterfront with colonial architecture facing futuristic skyscrapers.

Zhujiajiao Water Town

Venice of Shanghai - ancient canals, stone bridges, and traditional architecture.

🛂 China Visa Note:

Most nationalities need a visa for China. Apply in advance, or check if your country qualifies for transit visa exemption.

🏨 Suggested Accommodation:

Beijing: Hutong area courtyard hotel. Shanghai: Near The Bund or French Concession.

The world's most beautiful building. Visit at sunrise for smaller crowds and golden light.

Varanasi Extension

Egypt: Cairo & Luxor

📍 Cairo & Luxor, Egypt
Week 30

🏠 Base Locations: Cairo (4 days) → Luxor (3 days)

Massive temple complexes on the Nile. Karnak's hypostyle hall is mind-blowing.

Nile Felucca Ride

Traditional sailboat cruise on the Nile at sunset - pure magic.

Islamic Cairo

Al-Azhar Mosque, Khan el-Khalili bazaar, and medieval architecture.

✈️ Next Destination:

A short flight from Cairo takes you to Rome - welcome to Europe!

🏨 Suggested Accommodation:

Cairo: Hotel with Pyramid views in Giza. Luxor: East Bank or Nile cruise.
