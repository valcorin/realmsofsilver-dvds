Trip Overview

Experience the best of Japan on this comprehensive 16-day journey for three travelers. From the ultra-modern streets of Tokyo to ancient temples in Kyoto, the moving history of Hiroshima, and a stunning mountain hike in the Japanese Alps, this itinerary covers Japan's most iconic and historic sites. Staying in carefully selected mid-level boutique hotels, you'll enjoy authentic Japanese hospitality while exploring this fascinating country.

💡 Essential Tips for Japan Travel

  • Purchase a 14-day JR Pass before arriving in Japan - significant savings on bullet trains
  • Get a Suica or Pasmo IC card for local transportation
  • Download offline maps and translation apps (Google Translate with camera feature)
  • Carry cash - many smaller establishments don't accept cards
  • 7-Eleven ATMs are the most reliable for international cards
  • Remove shoes when entering temples, traditional restaurants, and hotels
  • Bow slightly when greeting people and receiving items
  • Don't tip - it's not customary and can cause confusion

Explore Tokyo - Traditional & Modern

📅 Full Day
📍 Tokyo

Activities & Attractions:

  • Morning: Visit Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa - Tokyo's oldest and most famous temple
  • Walk through Nakamise Shopping Street for traditional souvenirs
  • Take the subway to Shibuya - cross the famous Shibuya Crossing
  • Explore Harajuku's Takeshita Street for pop culture and street fashion
  • Visit Meiji Shrine - peaceful Shinto shrine in a forested park
  • Evening in Shinjuku - neon lights, Golden Gai bars, and dinner

⭐ Don't Miss:

The contrast between ancient Senso-ji Temple and the ultra-modern Shibuya Crossing perfectly captures Tokyo's unique blend of tradition and innovation. Visit Senso-ji early morning to avoid crowds!

🏨 Accommodation:

Continue stay at boutique hotel in Shinjuku or Asakusa area

4

Tokyo - Imperial History & Modern Culture

📅 Full Day
📍 Tokyo

Activities & Attractions:

  • Morning: Imperial Palace East Gardens - beautiful traditional Japanese gardens
  • Walk around the Imperial Palace moat and plaza
  • Visit the Tokyo National Museum in Ueno Park - Japan's oldest museum
  • Explore Ueno Park - temples, shrines, and cherry blossom trees
  • Afternoon: Akihabara Electric Town - anime, manga, and electronics heaven
  • Evening: TeamLab digital art museum or Tokyo Skytree for city views
  • Farewell Tokyo dinner in Ginza district

⭐ Historic Highlight:

The Tokyo National Museum houses over 110,000 objects including 89 National Treasures. Don't miss the Japanese Gallery for samurai armor, swords, and ancient art.

🏨 Accommodation:

Final night at Tokyo boutique hotel

5

Tokyo to Kyoto - Ancient Capital

📅 Travel & Explore
📍 Kyoto
🚄 Shinkansen: Tokyo Station → Kyoto Station (Nozomi or Hikari)
Duration: 2 hours 15 minutes
Note: JR Pass valid on Hikari and Kodama (not the faster Nozomi or Mizuho) 🗺️ Check Train Times

Afternoon in Kyoto:

  • Depart Tokyo early morning by Shinkansen
  • Arrive Kyoto and check into hotel
  • Afternoon: Fushimi Inari Shrine - iconic 10,000 vermillion torii gates
  • Hike partway up the mountain trail (2-3 hours round trip)
  • Evening stroll through Gion district - traditional geisha quarter
  • Dinner at a traditional kaiseki restaurant

⭐ Don't Miss:

Fushimi Inari is best visited early morning or late afternoon to avoid crowds and capture magical photos of the endless torii gates winding up the mountainside.

🏨 Boutique Hotel Recommendation:

Hotel Kanra Kyoto or Mitsui Garden Hotel Kyoto Shinmachi Bettei - Boutique ryokan-style hotels blending traditional and modern aesthetics. Private onsen options available. Triple rooms ¥25,000-40,000/night.

6

Kyoto - Golden Pavilion & Zen Gardens

📅 Full Day
📍 Kyoto

Activities & Attractions:

  • Morning: Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) - iconic gold-leaf covered temple
  • Ryoan-ji Temple - famous Zen rock garden, perfect for meditation
  • Lunch in the Arashiyama area
  • Arashiyama Bamboo Grove - walk through towering bamboo forest
  • Tenryu-ji Temple - UNESCO World Heritage zen temple
  • Cross the Togetsukyo Bridge at sunset
  • Evening: Return to central Kyoto for dinner

⭐ Historic Significance:

Kinkaku-ji was originally built in 1397 as a retirement villa for shogun Ashikaga Yoshimitsu. The top two floors are covered entirely in gold leaf, reflecting beautifully in the surrounding pond.

🏨 Accommodation:

Continue stay at Kyoto boutique hotel

7

Day Trip to Nara - Ancient Temples & Sacred Deer

📅 Day Trip
📍 Nara (from Kyoto)
🚃 Train: Kyoto → Nara (JR Nara Line or Kintetsu Railway)
Duration: 45 minutes
Note: JR Pass covers JR Nara Line

Day Trip Highlights:

  • Walk through Nara Park with over 1,200 friendly sacred deer
  • Visit Todai-ji Temple - housing one of Japan's largest bronze Buddha statues
  • Explore Kasuga Taisha Shrine - famous for thousands of stone and bronze lanterns
  • Stroll through the beautiful Isuien Garden
  • Visit Kofuku-ji Temple and its five-story pagoda
  • Sample local specialties like kakinoha-zushi (persimmon leaf sushi)
  • Return to Kyoto for evening

⭐ Don't Miss:

Todai-ji's Daibutsu (Great Buddha) is 15 meters tall and weighs approximately 500 tons. The Daibutsuden hall that houses it is one of the world's largest wooden buildings!

🏨 Accommodation:

Return to Kyoto boutique hotel

8

Kyoto - Eastern Hills & Tea Ceremony

📅 Full Day
📍 Kyoto

Activities & Attractions:

  • Morning: Kiyomizu-dera Temple - famous wooden terrace with city views
  • Walk down Ninenzaka and Sannenzaka - historic preserved streets
  • Visit Ginkaku-ji (Silver Pavilion) and its exquisite moss garden
  • Walk along the Philosopher's Path - scenic canal-side walk
  • Afternoon: Traditional tea ceremony experience
  • Explore Nishiki Market - "Kyoto's Kitchen" for food tastings
  • Evening: Final night in Kyoto - optional maiko performance

⭐ Cultural Experience:

A traditional tea ceremony (chado) is a quintessential Japanese cultural experience. Book in advance at Camellia or En tea house for an authentic ceremony with English explanation.

🏨 Accommodation:

Final night at Kyoto boutique hotel

9

Kyoto to Hiroshima - Peace & Reflection

📅 Travel & Explore
📍 Hiroshima
🚄 Shinkansen: Kyoto → Hiroshima (Sakura or Hikari)
Duration: 1 hour 40 minutes

Afternoon in Hiroshima:

  • Depart Kyoto morning and arrive Hiroshima by noon
  • Check into hotel and drop off luggage
  • Visit the Hiroshima Peace Memorial Park
  • See the A-Bomb Dome (Genbaku Dome) - UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum - moving exhibits on August 6, 1945
  • Cenotaph for the A-bomb Victims and Peace Flame
  • Evening: Try Hiroshima-style okonomiyaki in the Okonomi-mura food hall

⭐ Historic Importance:

The Peace Memorial Park stands at the epicenter of the atomic bomb blast. The experience is profound and moving, offering a powerful message about peace and the consequences of nuclear weapons.

🏨 Boutique Hotel Recommendation:

Sheraton Grand Hiroshima Hotel or Hotel Granvia Hiroshima - Comfortable hotels connected to or near Hiroshima Station. Triple rooms ¥18,000-30,000/night.

10

Miyajima Island - Floating Torii Gate

📅 Day Trip
📍 Miyajima Island (from Hiroshima)
🚃 + ⛴️: JR train to Miyajimaguchi (30 min) + Ferry (10 min)
Note: JR Pass covers both train and JR Ferry

Island Exploration:

  • Take morning train and ferry to Miyajima Island
  • See the iconic Itsukushima Shrine and floating torii gate
  • Walk at low tide to the base of the torii gate (check tide times!)
  • Hike to the top of Mount Misen for panoramic views (ropeway available)
  • Explore the charming shopping street - try momiji manju cakes
  • Meet the friendly wild deer roaming the island
  • Return to Hiroshima, then evening Shinkansen to Himeji

⭐ Don't Miss:

The floating torii gate of Itsukushima Shrine is one of Japan's most iconic images. At high tide it appears to float on water; at low tide you can walk right up to it. Plan your visit around tide times!

🚄 Evening Transfer: Hiroshima → Himeji (40 minutes by Shinkansen)
Stay overnight near Himeji for early castle visit tomorrow

🏨 Accommodation:

Hotel Nikko Himeji or Daiwa Roynet Hotel Himeji - Modern hotels near Himeji Station. ¥15,000-25,000/night for triple.

11

Himeji Castle & Journey to the Alps

📅 Travel Day
📍 Himeji → Takayama

Morning at Himeji Castle:

  • Early morning visit to Himeji Castle (opens 9am) - Japan's most spectacular original castle
  • Explore the castle's impressive architecture and defensive features
  • Walk through the adjacent Koko-en Garden
  • Allow 2-3 hours for the full castle experience

⭐ Historic Treasure:

Himeji Castle is Japan's finest surviving original castle, never destroyed by war, earthquake, or fire. The elegant white exterior has earned it the nickname "White Heron Castle." It's a UNESCO World Heritage Site and National Treasure.

🚄 Afternoon Journey: Himeji → Takayama
Route: Himeji → Shin-Osaka → Nagoya → Takayama
Total: Approximately 4 hours with transfers
Note: Scenic final leg on JR Hida Limited Express through mountains

🏨 Boutique Ryokan Recommendation:

Honjin Hiranoya Kachoan or Tanabe Ryokan - Traditional Japanese inns with tatami rooms, futons, and onsen baths. Experience authentic Japanese hospitality. ¥30,000-50,000/night including dinner and breakfast for three.

12

Takayama - Little Kyoto of the Alps

📅 Full Day
📍 Takayama

Activities & Attractions:

  • Early morning: Miyagawa Morning Market - local produce, crafts, and flowers
  • Explore Sanmachi Suji - preserved Edo-period merchant district
  • Visit sake breweries - taste locally brewed sake
  • Takayama Jinya - former government building from Edo period
  • Hida Folk Village - open-air museum of traditional thatched-roof farmhouses
  • Try Hida beef - considered among Japan's finest wagyu
  • Evening onsen at ryokan and traditional kaiseki dinner

⭐ Don't Miss:

Takayama's morning markets have been running for over 300 years. The Sanmachi Suji district has sake breweries marked by sugidama (cedar ball) decorations - a fresh green ball means new sake is ready!

🏨 Accommodation:

Continue stay at traditional ryokan in Takayama

13

Mountain Hike - Kamikochi Valley

📅 Hiking Day ⛰️
📍 Kamikochi, Japanese Alps
🚌 Transport: Bus from Takayama to Kamikochi (2 hours)
Note: Private vehicles not allowed - bus access only
First bus departs around 6:30am; last return bus around 5pm

🥾 Mountain Hiking Day

  • Difficulty: Easy to Moderate (depending on trail chosen)
  • Elevation: Valley floor at 1,500m; optional climbs to 2,000m+
  • Duration: 4-6 hours of hiking
  • Terrain: Well-maintained trails, wooden boardwalks, some rocky sections
  • What to bring: Hiking boots, layers, rain jacket, water, snacks, camera

🗺️ Kamikochi Trail Maps & Hiking Routes

Hiking Route Options:

  • Start at Kappa Bridge - iconic suspension bridge over crystal-clear Azusa River
  • Easy route: Taisho Pond to Kappa Bridge loop (2-3 hours)
  • Moderate route: Continue to Myojin Pond and Tokusawa (4-5 hours)
  • Challenging option: Climb to Dakesawa Hut for alpine views (6+ hours)
  • Enjoy stunning views of the Northern Alps peaks including Hotaka-dake
  • Spot wildlife: Japanese macaques, serow, and various birds
  • Pack a bento lunch to enjoy by the river

⭐ Mountain Highlight:

Kamikochi is often called the "Japanese Yosemite" - a pristine alpine valley surrounded by 3,000m peaks of the Northern Alps. The turquoise waters of the Azusa River and dramatic mountain backdrop create unforgettable scenery. This is one of Japan's most beautiful natural landscapes.

🚌 Evening Return: Bus back to Takayama (2 hours)
Relax in onsen after the hike!

🏨 Accommodation:

Final night at Takayama ryokan - enjoy the onsen after your mountain adventure!
